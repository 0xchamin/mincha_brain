> promoted to `../../brain/`. The **fact-checker** persona owns the verdicts; see `../../AGENTS.md`
> and `prd.md` §6.

> **Gate note for this source (read before citing any row).** This is **lecture 1 of a course**, so
> its natural output is a *taxonomy* and not a *result*. Where it does present numbers, they come
> from the lecturer's own lab's preprints (**T3**) narrated by their senior author, which is **not
> independent**, and the surrounding lecture is **T4**. The two legs available here are the slide and
> the narration, and on this source they agree unusually often - which proves the lecture is
> internally consistent and proves nothing about the world. **The most quotable slide in the source
> is also the one carrying a recorded divergence (`d1`), so read `n2` and `n3` as a pair or not at
> all.**
